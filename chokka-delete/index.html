Delete your Bangla Ludu account

Bangla Ludu (বাংলার লুডু) by Automata Studios

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Bangla Ludu and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Settings (bottom right of the home screen).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The account is deleted for good 7 days later. Until then you can change your mind: tap the button again (it now says Deletion pending) and choose Keep my account.

Or ask us by e-mail

If you cannot open the game, e-mail contact.automatastudios@gmail.com with the subject "Bangla Ludu account deletion" and your player name (and the e-mail address you sign in with, if any). We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your player profile: name, picture, level and XP
  • Coins, bought items (avatars, boards, tokens, sounds, movie clips, chat packs), VIP and remove-ads status
  • Friends, friend requests, recently-played players and your place in league lists
  • Game rewards history, sign-in sessions, the link to your Google or e-mail sign-in, and your notification token
  • On an e-mail request, also your e-mail sign-in record

What is kept

  • Purchase receipts (the store's purchase token and product name), no longer linked to any account, so the same purchase cannot be used twice. Your payment details are never seen by us; they stay with Google Play.
  • Feedback messages you sent us from the game, to improve the game. E-mail us if you want them deleted too.
  • Anonymous usage and crash statistics (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, by Google), which Google keeps for up to 14 months and does not tie to your name.
